**Full Description (4000 chars max):**

🕌 **Build Taqwa Through Daily Spiritual Habits**

Taqwa is your companion for building and maintaining Islamic habits with ease and consistency. Track your daily prayers, Quran reading, dhikr, and other spiritual practices in one beautiful, easy-to-use app.

📿 **KEY FEATURES**

✅ **Daily Habit Tracking**
- Track multiple Islamic practices daily
- Grade-based system (Excellent to Poor)
- Quick entry methods: Grid, List, Card, and Swipe views
- Track both current and past dates

📊 **Analytics & Progress**
- Visual charts showing your spiritual journey
- Streak tracking to maintain consistency
- Category-wise performance breakdown
- Achievement system to celebrate milestones

🎯 **Customizable Templates**
- Pre-built Islamic habit templates
- Create custom categories and habits
- Flexible item management
- Color-coded organization

☪️ **Islamic Features**
- Hijri calendar integration
- Prayer tracking (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
- Quran reading tracker
- Dhikr and Dua tracking
- Optional prayers (Tahajjud, Duha)
- Fasting tracker

🌙 **Beautiful Design**
- Dark and light theme support
- Intuitive Arabic/English interface
- Smooth animations
- Responsive design for all devices

☁️ **Cloud Sync**
- Secure Firebase backend
- Access your data from multiple devices
- Automatic backup
- Privacy-focused (your data stays yours)

🏆 **Achievements**
- First Step: Complete your first day
- Week Warrior: Maintain 7-day streak
- Perfect Day: Achieve 100% completion
- Consistency Master: 30-day streak
- Habit Builder: 100 total days
- Excellence: 10 perfect days

📈 **Why Taqwa?**

Building consistent Islamic habits is essential for spiritual growth. Taqwa makes it simple to track your daily practices, see your progress over time, and stay motivated with achievements and streaks.

Whether you're working on perfecting your five daily prayers, reading more Quran, or maintaining regular dhikr, Taqwa helps you build the consistency that leads to lasting change.

**Perfect for:**
- Muslims seeking to improve their daily practices
- Anyone wanting to build Islamic habits
- Students of knowledge tracking their progress
- Families teaching children about consistency

🔒 **Privacy & Security**
- End-to-end encrypted data storage
- No ads or tracking
- Your spiritual journey is private
- GDPR compliant

📱 **Start Your Journey Today**

Download Taqwa and begin building the habits that bring you closer to Allah. Track your progress, celebrate your achievements, and watch your spiritual consistency grow.
