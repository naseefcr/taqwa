## Introduction

Taqwa is an Islamic habit tracking application designed to help Muslims build and maintain spiritual practices through daily tracking and analytics. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account Information
- Email address (when you create an account)
- Authentication data (handled securely by Firebase Authentication)

### 2. Habit Tracking Data
- Your daily habit entries and completion records
- Category preferences and custom templates
- Progress statistics and achievement data
- Date and time of entries

### 3. Automatically Collected Information
- Device information (type, operating system)
- App usage analytics
- Error logs for debugging purposes

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

1. **Provide Core Functionality**
   - Store and sync your habit tracking data
   - Calculate statistics and achievements
   - Enable multi-device access to your data

2. **Improve the App**
   - Analyze app performance and fix bugs
   - Understand user behavior to enhance features
   - Provide customer support

3. **Account Management**
   - Authenticate and secure your account
   - Send important notifications (if enabled)
   - Prevent fraud and abuse

---

## Data Storage and Security

### Firebase & Cloud Storage
- All user data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore)
- Data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols
- Firebase Authentication manages user credentials securely
- We follow Firebase's security best practices

### Data Retention
- Your data is retained as long as your account is active
- You can request account deletion at any time by contacting us
- Upon account deletion, all personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days

We DO NOT:

- Share your habit data with advertisers
- Use your data for purposes other than app functionality

We may share information with:
